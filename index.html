The 4-Step Adoption Protocol

Engineered for an enduring, lifelong match. Our process marries clinical veterinary diagnostics with personalized environmental vetting to guarantee absolute synergy between companion and guardian.

01 15 MIN INTAKE

Lifestyle Architecture

Complete our comprehensive living profile assessing acoustic tolerance, daily rhythm, outdoor access, and co-resident dynamics.

AUTOMATED COMPATIBILITY AI
02 30 MIN SESSION

Virtual Sanctuary Meet

Live interactive video consultation directly with the pet's dedicated foster curator and an accredited veterinary behaviorist.

FULL MEDICAL HISTORY REVIEW
03 PRIVATE SALON

Direct Meet & Greet

Experience an unhurried, private sanctuary session in our tranquil acclimatization suites or arrange an accompanied courier visit.

CALIBRATED SENSORY SPACE
04 30-DAY GUARANTEE

Welcome Home & Escort

Handover includes international health passport, Trupanion policy initiation, microchip registration, and round-the-clock liaison access.

24/7 CLINICAL HOTLINE
01. Decompression Sanctuary

Prepare a quiet, low-stimulation room with orthopedic bedding, fresh filtered water, and familiar scent textiles for the first 72 hours.

02. Nutritional Continuity

Maintain the exact clinical diet provided in your welcome kit for 14 days before introducing gradual dietary transitions.

03. Veterinary Liaison Check-In

Complete your complimentary 7-day telehealth wellness review with your assigned sanctuary behaviorist.
